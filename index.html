Find where to buy or stream your songs

InfiniFind Music is a free, open-source desktop app. It reads the songs in your YouTube library and builds a clean list with links to the iTunes Store, Bandcamp, Amazon Music, Deezer and Spotify, plus the YouTube link of each song.

What it does

  • Reads your playlists and liked videos with read-only Google sign-in, or a Google Takeout export you download yourself.
  • Finds each song in the stores and checks whether its video is still available.
  • Saves the result as an HTML page and a text list, on your computer.

Your data stays on your computer

The app has no server that receives your data. It does not sell data, show ads or use analytics. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Open source

The code is public under the MIT license. Anyone can read it, check what the app does and report problems. See the repository.
