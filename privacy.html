Privacy Policy — InfiniFind Music

Effective date: 2026-11-09

Website: https://infinifind.app

Contact: contato@infinifind.app

InfiniFind Music ("the app") is a free, open-source desktop application. It helps you find where

to buy or stream the songs in your YouTube library and keeps a clean list of links. This policy

explains what the app does with your data.

1. Short version

  • The app runs on your computer. It has no server of its own that receives your data.
  • When you sign in with Google, the app reads your playlists, liked videos and video titles,

with read-only access (the youtube.readonly scope). It cannot change your account.

  • The app does not sell data, does not show ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and does

not use Google user data to train AI models.

  • You can sign out at any time. Signing out revokes the app's access at Google and deletes the

stored token from your computer.

2. Information the app accesses

When you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests one permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly: read-only access to your YouTube data.

Through the YouTube Data API the app reads:

  • the names of your playlists and their videos (IDs, titles, channel names);
  • your liked videos (count and list);
  • your channel name, to show "signed in as";
  • whether each video is available, private, deleted or blocked in your country.

The app does not read your watch history, subscriptions, comments, messages, email or any other

Google data.

If you do not sign in, you can still use Google Takeout exports that you download yourself. The

app reads those files only on your computer.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to build the lists and pages you request: matching each song to a store,

checking whether its video is still available, and creating the files you asked for. It is not

used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the features you use.

4. Where the data is stored

  • Google sign-in token: stored in your operating system's keychain (macOS Keychain, Windows

Credential Manager or Secret Service on Linux), when available. It is never sent to the

developer, to a web page or into a generated file.

  • Client file: the OAuth client file you import is saved in the app-data folder, readable only

by your user account.

  • Search cache: song titles, store matches and availability results are kept in plain text in

your system cache folder (for example ~/Library/Caches/InfiniFindMusic on macOS). The cache is

deleted automatically 30 days after it was created. You can delete it at any time.

  • Generated files: the HTML pages and text lists are saved where you choose on your computer.

5. Data sent to other services

  • Google (sign-in and YouTube Data API): requests needed to read your playlists and check video

availability. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

  • YouTube: when you are not signed in, the app reads public YouTube video pages to check

availability. Pages are requested from your computer, the same way a browser would.

  • Apple iTunes Search API: the artist and song title of each song are sent to find its

iTunes Store page. No account is involved.

  • Store search links: the app creates links to search pages (for example Bandcamp, Amazon

Music, Deezer and Spotify). These sites receive a search only when you click the link.

The app sends no other data to any server operated by the developer.

6. Google user data and the Limited Use requirements

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the

Google API Services User Data Policy,

including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the features described in this policy.
  • Google user data is not transferred to others, except as needed to provide those features

(the Google and iTunes requests above), to comply with the law, or with your consent.

  • Google user data is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop or train AI models.
  • Humans do not read your Google user data, except with your consent, for security purposes, or

to comply with the law. The developer has no access to it, because it stays on your computer.

The app also follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service

and the YouTube Terms of Service.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

  • In the app: click Sign out. The app revokes its access at Google and deletes the token.
  • At Google: you can also remove the app at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • On your computer: delete the app, its cache folder and its app-data folder. The keychain

entry is removed when you sign out.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the effective date is

updated. Significant changes are described in the project's changelog.

10. Open source

The source code is public under the MIT license at https://github.com/jucabalasilva/infinifind-music.

You can check every statement above in the code.
